Boost Your FPS in Fortnite



Welcome to the Boost FPS section! Here, you'll find tips and tricks to enhance your gaming performance by increasing your frames per second (FPS) in Fortnite.

Follow these steps to optimize your settings and get the best possible performance:

  • Adjust In-Game Settings: Lower your graphics settings such as shadows, textures, and effects to improve FPS.
  • Update Graphics Drivers: Ensure your GPU drivers are up-to-date for optimal performance.
  • Close Background Applications: Free up system resources by closing unnecessary applications running in the background.
  • Optimize Windows Settings: Disable visual effects and set your power plan to high performance.
  • Use Performance Mode: Enable Performance Mode in Fortnite settings for a significant FPS boost.

Additional Tips



  • Overclock Your GPU: If you're comfortable with it, overclocking your GPU can provide a performance boost.
  • Install Game on SSD: Installing Fortnite on a solid-state drive (SSD) can reduce load times and improve performance.
  • Regular Maintenance: Keep your system clean from dust and ensure proper cooling to prevent thermal throttling.

OS Settings on Windows Operator



By following these tips and optimizing your settings, you should see a noticeable improvement in your FPS while playing Fortnite. Happy gaming!



  • Disable Fullscreen Optimizations: Right-click on the Fortnite executable, go to Properties > Compatibility, and check "Disable fullscreen optimizations".
  • Set High Priority: Open Task Manager, find Fortnite in the Processes tab, right-click it, and set its priority to "High".
  • Adjust Virtual Memory: Increase your virtual memory (paging file) size to help with performance during intensive gaming sessions.
  • Set your energy saving settings in widows menu setting to maximum performance: Increase maximum performance in Windows power options and you will be able to get +200FPS guarantee.
