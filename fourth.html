Understanding ZERO Ping



ZERO Ping refers to achieving the lowest possible latency in your network connection, which is essential for a smooth gaming experience. A low ping means that your actions are transmitted to the game server and back with minimal delay.

To achieve ZERO Ping, consider the following tips:

  • Use a Wired Connection: A wired Ethernet connection is more stable and faster than Wi-Fi, reducing latency significantly.
  • Choose the Right Server: Select game servers that are geographically closer to you to minimize ping and delay.
  • Optimize your Network settings: Go to settings, ethernet and choose DNS IPv4 path the use 1.1.1.1 and second path 1.0.0.1.

Best Practices for Maintaining ZERO Ping



To maintain a consistent low ping, follow these best practices:

  • Keep Your Router Updated: Regularly update your router's firmware to ensure optimal performance and security.
  • Use Quality Cables: Invest in high-quality Ethernet cables to avoid signal degradation and interference.
  • Monitor Network Usage: Keep an eye on how many devices are connected to your network and their bandwidth usage.
  • Restart Your Network Equipment: Periodically restart your modem and router to clear any temporary issues or congestion.
