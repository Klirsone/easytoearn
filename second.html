Best settings to build faster and better



To optimize your building speed and efficiency in Fortnite, consider adjusting the following settings:

  • Keybinds: Customize your keybinds for building to ensure they are comfortable and allow for quick access. Many pro players use keys that are easy to reach without moving their fingers too far from movement keys.
  • Sensitivity: Adjust your mouse or controller sensitivity to find a balance that allows for quick turns and precise building placement. Higher sensitivity can help with faster building but may reduce accuracy.
  • Building Edit Hold Time: Set the building edit hold time to the lowest possible setting (0.00 seconds) to allow for instant edits.
  • Turbo Building: Enable turbo building to allow continuous placement of building pieces by holding down the build button.
  • HUD Scale: Adjust the HUD scale to ensure that important information is visible without obstructing your view of the game.


Is it all just training and focus on your improvement?



Consistency is key when it comes to improving your building skills in Fortnite. Here are some practice routines to help you get better:

  • Daily Warm-Up: Spend 10-15 minutes each day in a creative map focusing on basic building techniques like ramps, walls, and 90s.
  • 1v1 Build Fights: Engage in regular 1v1 build fights with friends or other players to practice building under pressure.
  • Editing Drills: Dedicate time to practicing edits on various structures to improve speed and accuracy.
  • Scenario Practice: Create or join maps that simulate real-game scenarios, such as high ground retakes or box fights.
  • Review and Reflect: Record your practice sessions and review them to identify areas for improvement and track your progress over time.


Additional Tips



  • Stay Calm: Building quickly can be stressful, especially in high-pressure situations. Practice staying calm and focused to improve your building under pressure.
  • Watch Pros: Study professional players to learn advanced building techniques and strategies.
  • Use Visual Aids: Consider using visual aids like keybind overlays or building guides to help you remember important techniques.
  • Take Breaks: Avoid burnout by taking regular breaks during practice sessions to rest and recharge.
